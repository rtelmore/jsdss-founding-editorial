Dear Colleagues,

It is our great privilege to introduce the
\href{https://jsds-sports.github.io/}{\emph{Journal of Statistics and
Data Science in Sports}} (\emph{JSDSS}). We write to you not only to
announce a new publication venue, but to share a founding philosophy
that we believe is long overdue in our field. \emph{JSDSS} is born out
of a shared frustration with the increasingly exploitative dynamics of
academic publishing, and a collective conviction that there is a better
way forward. We are confident that many of you share this frustration.

As professors, editors, reviewers, and contributors to the academic
literature, we have watched the pay-to-play model of open-access
publishing grow from an occasional inconvenience into a structural
barrier. Publishers have found clever ways to monetize visibility
itself. It is not uncommon to encounter promotional language such as:

\begin{quote}
``Articles published open access with {[}publisher redacted{]} typically
receive 35\% more citations and over 5 times as many downloads compared
to those that are not published open access.''
\end{quote}

Statements like these represent a troubling commodification of scholarly
impact that conflates purchased exposure with genuine scientific
contribution. We believe the academic community deserves better.
Accordingly, \emph{JSDSS} is built on a different foundation: the
conviction that high-quality sports statistics and data science research
should be freely accessible to readers and free to publish for authors,
without exception.

We, the five founding editors, share this vision and are committed to
building a journal that reflects the values of academic rigor,
reproducibility, and openness. We invite you to join us on this journey.

\section{Founding Vision}\label{founding-vision}

\emph{JSDSS} was founded on three core principles.

First, our commitment to open access is more than just lip service. At
\emph{JSDSS}, open access means \textbf{free} to read and \textbf{free}
to publish---no exceptions. \emph{JSDSS} is a
\href{https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diamond_open_access}{Diamond Open
Access} journal. Research supported by academic institutions, public
funding, or personal effort should not require a payment to reach the
audience it deserves.

Second, reproducibility is essential and not an afterthought. The
credibility of sports analytics research depends on our ability to
verify, replicate, and build upon earlier work. \emph{JSDSS} will
actively incentivize transparency in data, code, and methodology, and
work that meets our reproducibility standards will receive a special
designation recognizing this commitment.

Third, we believe sport is a rich and underutilized laboratory for
statistical and data science innovation. From player evaluation and
in-game strategy to league design and fan engagement, sports data
present compelling, real-world problems that demand rigorous and
creative analytical thinking. We intend for \emph{JSDSS} to be the
definitive venue for this work.

\section{Aims and Scope}\label{aims-and-scope}

\subsection{Aims}\label{aims}

The aim of the \emph{Journal of Statistics and Data Science in Sports}
is to provide an outlet for original, rigorous, practical,
state-of-the-art, reproducible, and peer-reviewed analysis of sports
data as well as the data science tools (software, applications, data,
etc.) that support those analyses. The \emph{Journal of Statistics and
Data Science in Sports} will always be fully open to both readers and
authors: there are no fees associated with publishing or accessing
articles in \emph{JSDSS}.\\
Submissions will be required to follow the
\href{https://jasa-acs.github.io/repro-guide/}{Journal of the American
Statistical Association protocols for reproducibility}.

\subsection{Scope}\label{scope}

We define sports broadly to include any nondeterministic competition or
game. Examples include basketball, soccer, badminton as well as
competitions like chess and eSports.

We welcome submissions that analyze any aspect of sports data in
appropriate and innovative ways. These analyses may include, but are not
limited to:

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  In--game strategy, player acquisition, and player and team ratings and
  evaluation,
\item
  Predicting play, game, or season outcomes, analysis of league
  structures and rules,
\item
  Analysis of player-tracking data,
\item
  Any other analyses that would help teams, players, fans, leagues,
  media and researchers understand the game better or make
  more-informated decisions.
\end{itemize}

We also accept timely review articles and manuscripts discussing novel
datasets, innovative software or applications and original applications
of methodology to sports data. Applications should be focused on
relevant questions whose answers are likely to be actionable by players,
coaches, managers, and/or league officials. \emph{JSDSS} is committed to
making all articles about the analysis of sports data freely available.
Additionally, reproducibility is strongly encouraged particularly when
non-proprietary data is used, and articles that meet our standards of
reproducibility will receive a special designation.

\section{\texorpdfstring{What Makes \emph{JSDSS}
Special}{What Makes JSDSS Special}}\label{what-makes-jsdss-special}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Genuinely free}: Unlike many open-access outlets, \emph{JSDSS}
  charges no article processing fees. Publishing and reading are
  entirely free.
\item
  \textbf{Reproducibility recognition}: Articles that meet our
  reproducibility standards --- sharing data, code, and analytic
  workflows --- receive a special designation visible in the journal.
\item
  \textbf{Broad article types}: In addition to traditional research
  papers, \emph{JSDSS} welcomes review articles, data notes,
  software/application papers, and rapid communications.
\item
  \textbf{Community focus}: \emph{JSDSS} is built by and for the sports
  statistics community. We welcome contributions from academics and
  practitioners alike, and we are committed to constructive, timely peer
  review.
\item
  \textbf{Best-in-class open platforms}: \emph{JSDSS} is published by
  the \href{https://library.charlotte.edu/}{J. Murrey Atkins Library} at
  the \href{https://www.charlotte.edu/}{University of North Carolina
  Charlotte}, who is also providing backend support for our use of the
  \href{https://pkp.sfu.ca/software/ojs/}{Open Journal Systems}
  platform. We are partnering with
  \href{https://projecteuclid.org/}{Project Euclid} to disseminate our
  content, and will be experimenting with reproducibility reviews using
  \href{https://github.com/jsds-sports}{GitHub}.
\end{itemize}

\section{Peer Review and Publishing
Ethics}\label{peer-review-and-publishing-ethics}

\emph{JSDSS} adheres to the highest ethical standards. All submitted
manuscripts are subject to initial review by the editorial team, and
those found suitable will undergo peer review by independent, anonymous
expert referees. We are committed to timely review and constructive
feedback. Our goal is to make \emph{JSDSS} a venue where authors feel
their work will be treated with fairness, expertise, and respect.

\section{The Future}\label{the-future}

Sports analytics is a field that sits at the intersection of statistics
and data science, and genuine public enthusiasm. We believe that
manuscripts published in \emph{JSDSS} have the potential to reach not
just our academic community, but front offices, coaching staffs, and
fans alike. That possibility is part of what makes this field exciting,
and it is part of what motivates us to build a publishing home that
aligns with our vision.

We warmly invite researchers, practitioners, and innovators to
contribute their best work to \emph{JSDSS}. We also invite feedback as
we build this journal together. Together, we can demonstrate that
high-quality, open, reproducible science and academic publishing are not
mutually exclusive, but rather are complementary.

We look forward to working with you!
